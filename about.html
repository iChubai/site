About Me

I am an undergraduate student at Nankai University with a strong passion for computer vision and machine learning. I have gained research experience as a Research Intern at the VCIP Lab at Nankai University, where I work with Professor Ming-Ming Cheng and Associate Professor Qibin Hou, and at NTU.

I am also a member of OpenEnvision, where I am happy to work with Juanxi Tian. OpenEnvision is a joint research hub advancing open vision intelligence through academia-industry collaboration. We work closely with academic partners such as UC San Diego, UIUC, Princeton, NTU, and MMLab, as well as industry collaborators including Nvidia, Adobe, Meta, and ByteDance Seed. We continue to expand our impact through open-source projects and knowledge sharing.

Outside of research, I like rap music. My favorite Chinese rapper is Asen, and my favorite foreign rappers are J. Cole and Kendrick Lamar.
